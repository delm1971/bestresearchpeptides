Analytical Methodology

How to Verify Research Peptide Purity & Quality in 2026

Sourcing unverified reagents introduces experimental artifacts and invalidates assay reproducibility. Use our four-point laboratory verification checklist before procuring any lyophilized peptide.

01

HPLC Purity Chromatograms

High-Performance Liquid Chromatography (HPLC) separates target peptides from synthesis deletion sequences and residual reagents. Always require a dominant single peak representing ≥98.5%–99%+ area under the curve.

02

Mass Spectrometry (LC-MS)

While HPLC measures purity percentage, Mass Spectrometry confirms molecular identity. Verify that the observed Dalton (Da) mass-to-charge peak matches the theoretical amino acid sequence weight.

03

Third-Party COA Traceability

In-house test sheets can be fabricated. Top suppliers like NovaGlo Labs and Merit Sciences publish third-party COAs with verifiable task numbers and lot codes matching the physical vial label.

04

Lyophilization & Cold Storage

Properly freeze-dried peptides appear as an intact white lyophilized puck under vacuum seal. Store un-reconstituted vials at -20°C protected from UV light to prevent oxidation and deamidation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Authentic COAs are issued by independent, ISO/IEC 17025 accredited analytical facilities rather than solely internal vendor spreadsheets. Look for a unique verification task code or QR link on the report, verify that the lot number matches your sealed vial, and confirm that both HPLC purity (%) and LC-MS molecular weight spectra are present.
For reproducible in-vitro receptor binding and biochemical studies, research-grade peptides should test at 98.0% purity or higher via HPLC. Every supplier ranked in our top 12 directory maintains average batch purities between 99.1% and 99.6%.
Peptides suspended in liquid form undergo rapid hydrolytic cleavage and microbial degradation at ambient temperatures. Lyophilization sublimates water content under high vacuum, producing an anhydrous crystalline cake that remains stable during domestic transit and can be stored long-term at -20°C.
